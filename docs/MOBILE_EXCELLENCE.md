# Making JagX Mobile One of the Best

This document captures the concrete ambitions that separate a “working mobile OS” from a **great** one.

## Measurable Targets (Aspirational)

| Area                    | Target                                      |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Touch latency           | Competitive with or better than leading OSes |
| Frame timing            | Consistent, low jitter even under load      |
| Cold launch of core apps| Extremely fast                              |
| Idle battery drain      | Industry-leading                            |
| Update size & reliability | Small, robust, A/B style where possible   |
| Privacy                 | No mandatory cloud identity, transparent permissions |
| Longevity               | Devices remain useful and updatable for many years |

## Cultural Rules for the Project

1. Every major feature is evaluated for its impact on mobile latency, power, and privacy.
2. “It works on desktop” is not sufficient justification for a design that hurts mobile.
3. Complexity that does not serve the user experience or security is rejected.
4. We prefer simple, auditable designs over clever ones that are hard to reason about.

## Long-term Bet

A smaller, coherent, from-scratch system can eventually deliver a more refined and trustworthy mobile experience than systems that carry decades of legacy and competing business interests.

That is the bet JagX is making.
